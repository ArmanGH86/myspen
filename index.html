My S-Pen

    Your phone's pen, on your computer

    What it does

    Real pressure

    Pressure levels, tilt and the pen button — drawing apps see a real pen tablet.

    Hover

    The cursor follows the pen before it touches the screen.

    Multi-touch

    Fingers work as a touchscreen, with palm rejection while you draw.

    Wi-Fi, USB or Bluetooth

    Connect the way you like — the connection stays stable.

    Private

    Optional password and hidden mode. Your pen data never leaves your local network.

    Your language

    English is built in; other languages download automatically inside the apps.

    Get started

    1. 1Install My S-Pen on your computer and open it.
    2. 2Install the app on your phone or tablet.
    3. 3Join the same Wi-Fi and pick your computer in the app — done.
